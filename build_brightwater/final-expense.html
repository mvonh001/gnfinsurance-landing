Straight answers

What does final expense insurance actually cover? It pays cash directly to the person you choose — typically used for the funeral and burial or cremation, final medical bills, and any small debts, so nothing lands on your family. The average funeral in Florida runs well into five figures; this is the policy sized for exactly that.

Can I qualify with health problems? Usually, yes. These policies are designed for people 50–85 and use simple health questions instead of exams. Different health situations fit different options — that's exactly what the phone call sorts out honestly.

Is this a sales call I'll regret? No. You'll talk to Michael — a licensed independent Florida agent, not a call center. If the honest answer is that you don't need this, that's the answer you'll get.
