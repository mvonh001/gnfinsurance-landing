Final expense insurance, explained honestly

Final expense insurance (people also call it burial insurance or funeral insurance) is a small whole life policy, usually $5,000 to $25,000, built to cover funeral costs, medical bills, and the loose ends a family faces after a death. The average funeral in Florida runs $8,000 to $12,000 before a headstone or cemetery costs. This coverage exists so your family never has to pass a hat.

What does burial insurance cost per month?

It depends on your age, whether you use tobacco, and the coverage amount. Most healthy Florida seniors between 60 and 80 land somewhere between $30 and $130 a month for $10,000 to $15,000 of coverage. Your exact rate depends on a few health questions and the carrier, which is why the quiz above shows you an honest range and the exact number comes from a licensed agent, not a form.

No medical exam, and the rate locks

These policies use a few health questions instead of an exam. Once issued, the premium never goes up and the coverage never shrinks, as long as you pay the premium. Many carriers offer guaranteed acceptance versions for people with serious health history, at a higher cost and usually with a two year waiting period. An honest agent tells you which type actually fits before you buy either one.

Common questions

Is this the same as the "$25,000 state program" in the ads? No. There is no government burial program. Those ads sell this same insurance with marketing on top. We wrote a whole page about it: the truth about the final expense "program".

Can I buy it for a parent or spouse? Yes, with their consent and signature. That is one of the most common ways these policies get bought, and the quiz above has options for exactly that.
